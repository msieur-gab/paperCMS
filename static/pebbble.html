Pebbble - Crafting Presence Through Code

Pebbble is a design-driven project born from a deeply human need — to preserve the irreplaceable bond of a parent's voice across physical and digital divides. It blends ancient ritual, modern cryptography, and crafted hardware into a secure, tactile experience. Drawing from Japanese joinery philosophy, it redefines technology as a vessel of presence, resilience, and love.
