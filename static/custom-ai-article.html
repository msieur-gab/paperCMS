The Case for Custom AI

While large language models promise universal solutions, their limitations in specialized contexts make a compelling case for custom AI development. This article explores how building tailored models that embrace uncertainty and continuous learning can better serve specific business needs, ensure cost control, and maintain compliance while avoiding the pitfalls of false confidence inherent in general-purpose AI solutions.
